# TSA Tourism Economics & Value Accounting Standards

This skill governs economic modeling, value-added calculations, and TSA accounting rules for the **Malaysia Tourism Value Optimizer** project.

## 1. Core Economic Framework & Strategic Shift

* **Paradigm Shift**: Transition from volume expansion (`more visitors`) to value capture (`more economic value from existing visitors`).
* **Dimension**: Focus exclusively on the **economic dimension of sustainable tourism** (SDG 8.9 and SDG 12.b). Do not claim complete measurement of environmental or social sustainability.
* **Core Hypothesis**: Accommodation and Length of Stay (ALOS) are pivotal conversion levers. Accommodation is an empirical hypothesis to validate against TSA data, not a foregone conclusion.

---

## 2. Non-Negotiable Formulas & Definitions

### A. Value-Added Intensity (VAI)
The primary efficiency metric measuring how much Gross Value Added (GVA) is generated per unit of gross output/supply:

$$VAI_{i,t} = \frac{\text{GVA}_{i,t}}{\text{DomesticSupply}_{i,t}}$$

* $i$: Tourism activity/industry/product.
* $t$: Year (2015–2025).
* Interpretation: The proportion of gross domestic supply represented by value added. A higher VAI indicates lower intermediate consumption leakages.

### B. Estimated Tourism-Attributable GVA (Proxy)
When estimating the value added directly generated by tourism consumption:

$$\text{EstimatedTourismGVA}_{i,t} = \text{ITC}_{i,t} \times VAI_{i,t}$$

Equivalently, under proportional allocation:

$$\text{EstimatedTourismGVA}_{i,t} = \text{IndustryGVA}_{i,t} \times \text{TourismRatio}_{i,t}$$

> **CRITICAL GUARDRAIL**:
> 1. **NEVER** label this analytical proxy as official product-level TDGVA.
> 2. Always label it as: `Estimated tourism-attributable GVA` or `Tourism value-added proxy`.
> 3. **NEVER** multiply an already tourism-attributable GVA by the Tourism Ratio a second time.

### C. Tourism Ratio
The official TSA share of relevant domestic supply consumed by visitors:

$$\text{TourismRatio}_{i,t} = \frac{\text{ITC}_{i,t}}{\text{DomesticSupply}_{i,t}}$$

### D. Internal Tourism Consumption (ITC) vs Survey Totals
* **TSA ITC**: Official macroeconomic measure comprising domestic tourism expenditure + inbound tourism expenditure + internal tourism consumption components.
* **DTS Total**: Domestic Tourism Survey expenditure aggregate.
* **Rule**: Do NOT treat DTS survey totals as conceptually identical to TSA ITC. Do not force reconciliation where accounting perimeters differ.

### E. Accommodation Performance Yield Metrics
When variables are compatible:

$$\text{AccommodationShare} = \frac{\text{AccommodationExpenditure}}{\text{TotalTourismExpenditure}}$$

$$\text{AccommodationSpendPerVisitor} = \frac{\text{AccommodationExpenditure}}{\text{DomesticVisitors}}$$

$$\text{AccommodationSpendPerTourist} = \frac{\text{AccommodationExpenditure}}{\text{OvernightTourists}}$$

> **Rule**: Total domestic visitors include both same-day excursionists and overnight tourists. Never mix total visitors and overnight tourists in the denominator without explicit label distinction.

## 3. Periodization for Longitudinal Analysis (2015–2025)

When computing averages, medians, coefficients of variation (CV), and compound growth trends, divide the 10-year horizon into:

1. **Pre-COVID (`2015–2019`)**: Structural baseline for mature demand and steady-state VAI.
2. **Disruption & Recovery (`2020–2022`)**: COVID shock period. Treat as an anomaly; do NOT let this distort long-run structural conclusions.
3. **Post-Recovery (`2023–2025`)**: The new operational reality, current cost structures, and recent supply-chain changes.

## 4. Empirical Product Classification Matrix (Stage B)

Do not rank products by VAI alone. Classify products into a 2x2 strategic quadrant:

| Quadrant | Criteria | Strategic Implication | Example / Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High-Value Core Activity** | High VAI + High ITC Scale | Protect, deepen, and anchor national yield. | Accommodation / F&B (if validated). |
| **High-Efficiency Growth Opportunity** | High VAI + Low ITC Scale | Expansion target; high yield per RM of visitor spend. | Specific recreation / cultural services. |
| **Efficiency-Improvement Priority** | Low VAI + High ITC Scale | Optimize supply chain; high leakage/intermediate cost. | Transport / Retail (shopping). |
| **Lower Strategic Priority** | Low VAI + Low ITC Scale | Low immediate economic transformation priority. | Low-intensity peripheral services. |

## 5. Specific Accounting Nuances & Structural Cases

### Travel Agencies and Reservation Services (2025 Dynamics)
* **What Happened**: In 2025, GVA grew, but Domestic Supply increased at a substantially faster rate. ITC grew strongly while the tourism ratio was relatively stable. Consequently, the mathematical ratio $VAI = GVA / Supply$ fell.
* **Interpretation Rule**: Do **not** state that the travel agency industry "collapsed" or shrank in 2025.
* **Valid Hypotheses**: The VAI contraction reflects higher intermediate consumption costs, platform commissions, cross-border digital booking systems, and low-margin bulk ticketing normalization.

---

## 6. Language & Claims Guardrails

* Never claim accommodation *causes* TDGVA growth (correlation $\neq$ causation).
* Never state an increase in ALOS *guarantees* RM X economic impact.
* Use approved analytical phrasing:
  * `associated with`
  * `value-added intensive`
  * `tourism value-added proxy`
  * `potential economic yield opportunity`
  * `scenario estimate`
